Company Financials API: UK, Denmark & Norway accounts + LEI

On Apify Store

Get normalized financials of UK, Danish and Norwegian companies, including private ones, straight from the official accounts they file with Companies House (UK), Erhvervsstyrelsen (Denmark) and the Brønnøysund Register of Company Accounts (Norway): revenue, gross profit, operating profit, net income, equity, net assets, current assets, cash, creditors and employees. Every number comes with the exact fact or field it was taken from, so you can audit any value. Each row also gets the company's LEI from GLEIF when it has one. Look up companies by company number, CVR or organisation number, or get the newest filings. For credit checks, KYB, M&A and lead research, and for AI agents.

Coverage

UK: accounts filed electronically with Companies House in the last ~60 days. Denmark: every annual report filed digitally with Erhvervsstyrelsen, all years. Norway: the last 3 fiscal years of every company that files accounts with the Brønnøysund Register (company accounts, not group accounts). Belgium only when the Actor owner has configured access. LEI looked up at GLEIF.

Input fields

FieldTypeMeaning
countriesarrayRegisters to read, as codes: GB (Companies House), DK (Erhvervsstyrelsen), NO (Brønnøysund Regnskapsregisteret), BE (National Bank of Belgium; needs enterprise numbers and NBB access set up by the Actor owner). Default ["GB", "DK", "NO"]. Used only when no numbers are given (it then returns each country's newest filings); with numbers, the countries of those numbers are read and this is ignored.
companyNumbersarrayUK Companies House numbers, e.g. ["03117531", "SC123456"] (shorter numbers are zero-padded to 8). Accounts of any year filed digitally (iXBRL), newest first; paper/PDF-only filings are skipped. Empty = the newest UK filings (last ~60 days).
cvrNumbersarrayDanish CVR numbers, 8 digits, e.g. ["24256790"]. All years are available, newest first. Empty = the newest Danish filings.
orgNumbersarrayNorwegian organisation numbers, 9 digits, e.g. ["923609016"]. The last 3 fiscal years are available, newest first. Empty = companies whose accounts were registered in the last 2 days.
enterpriseNumbersarrayBelgian KBO/BCE enterprise numbers, 10 digits with or without dots, e.g. ["0403.170.701"]. Required for BE (there is no "newest filings" mode for Belgium), which works only when the Actor owner has set up NBB access.
enrichLeibooleantrue (default) = look up each company's Legal Entity Identifier at GLEIF by its registration number and add lei and lei_status (null when the company has no LEI). false = skip the lookup.
maxResultsintegerMaximum company-years per country, newest filings first, e.g. 1 for a company's latest accounts. Default 5. One row per company per fiscal year.

Sources, licence and limits

Are the numbers in thousands? No. All values are in full currency units: the Actor applies each fact's scale and sign as tagged.

Why is revenue empty? The company didn't publish it (common for small UK and Danish companies, see above), or tagged it with a non-standard concept. metrics_found tells you how complete a row is.

Can I get older UK accounts? Yes, by company number: every year with digital (iXBRL) accounts. Older paper or PDF-only filings have no data to read and are skipped.

Why is lei null? The company has no LEI (most small companies don't), or GLEIF has it under a different registration number. lei_status shows whether an LEI found is still ISSUED or e.g. LAPSED (not renewed).

Are Norwegian figures consolidated? No: rows hold the company's own accounts. Equinor's row, for example, is the parent company, not the group.

Is Belgium supported? Only when the Actor owner has configured access to the National Bank of Belgium's web services; otherwise BE is skipped with a log message and costs nothing.

Personal data? Rows contain company-level figures and the company name only. Names of directors, board members, auditors or meeting chairs, addresses and free-text notes are never output. Norwegian sole proprietorships are skipped.

Legal basis? The accounts are public register filings. See LEGAL.md in the source for the terms checked.
