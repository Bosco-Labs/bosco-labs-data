Company Financials API: UK, Denmark & Norway accounts + LEI

Company financials (revenue, profit, equity, assets, cash, employees) of private and public companies from official accounts: UK Companies House (all digital years), Denmark (all years), Norway Brønnøysund (last 3 years).

Look up by company number, CVR or org number; adds LEI and a source per value.

Coverage

UK: accounts filed electronically with Companies House in the last ~60 days. Denmark: every annual report filed digitally with Erhvervsstyrelsen, all years. Norway: the last 3 fiscal years of every company that files accounts with the Brønnøysund Register (company accounts, not group accounts). Belgium only when the Actor owner has configured access. LEI looked up at GLEIF.

Price

Pricing (pay per event): $0.04 per company-year ($40 per 1,000 rows). Rows where the filing tags no figures at all (e.g. some dormant companies) are skipped and not charged. The Actor stops at your max charge limit.

Input fields

FieldTypeMeaning
countriesarrayRegisters to read, as codes: GB (Companies House), DK (Erhvervsstyrelsen), NO (Brønnøysund Regnskapsregisteret), BE (National Bank of Belgium; needs enterprise numbers and NBB access set up by the Actor owner). Default ["GB", "DK", "NO"]. Used only when no numbers are given (it then returns each country's newest filings); with numbers, the countries of those numbers are read and this is ignored.
companyNumbersarrayUK Companies House numbers, e.g. ["03117531", "SC123456"] (shorter numbers are zero-padded to 8). Accounts of any year filed digitally (iXBRL), newest first; paper/PDF-only filings are skipped. Empty = the newest UK filings (last ~60 days).
cvrNumbersarrayDanish CVR numbers, 8 digits, e.g. ["24256790"]. All years are available, newest first. Empty = the newest Danish filings.
orgNumbersarrayNorwegian organisation numbers, 9 digits, e.g. ["923609016"]. The last 3 fiscal years are available, newest first. Empty = companies whose accounts were registered in the last 2 days.
enterpriseNumbersarrayBelgian KBO/BCE enterprise numbers, 10 digits with or without dots, e.g. ["0403.170.701"]. Required for BE (there is no "newest filings" mode for Belgium), which works only when the Actor owner has set up NBB access.
enrichLeibooleantrue (default) = look up each company's Legal Entity Identifier at GLEIF by its registration number and add lei and lei_status (null when the company has no LEI). false = skip the lookup.
maxResultsintegerMaximum company-years per country, newest filings first, e.g. 1 for a company's latest accounts. Default 5. One row per company per fiscal year.

Use with AI agents

Add this URL to your MCP client (Claude, Cursor, VS Code, ...) to load the Actor as a tool through the Apify hosted MCP server. It signs you in with OAuth or accepts Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>.

https://mcp.apify.com?tools=bosco_labs/company-accounts

Example prompt: “"What were Novo Nordisk's revenue and equity in its latest Danish annual report?"” The agent passes:

{"cvrNumbers": ["24256790"], "maxResults": 1}

stable, typed JSON, one row per company per fiscal year. Every row has schema_version and a source_url pointing to the official filing or register record (plus registry_url and a sources entry per value), so the agent can cite its source. With registration numbers, only their countries are read, so a lookup never returns other companies; bad numbers fail at once with a valid example.

Or call the Apify API: POST https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/bosco_labs~company-accounts/run-sync-get-dataset-items with the input as JSON body. Cost: $0.04 per company-year.
