Bosco Labs data APIs

Clean, typed JSON from official sources, as Apify Actors you can call by API or from an AI agent over MCP. Priced per row delivered.

Machine-readable index: llms.txt (summary) and llms-full.txt (all input and output fields). All Actors on the Bosco Labs Apify Store profile.

Finance & filings

  • SEC Insider Trading API (Form 4): live + history since 2006

    Every insider purchase and sale reported on SEC Form 4 (directors, officers, 10% owners), from official EDGAR filings, 2006 to today, with cluster-buy and cluster-sell signals; filter by ticker, CIK, transaction code, role, value and date.

    $0.0018 per transaction row and $0.02 per cluster signal · Apify Store

  • SEC EDGAR Filings API: 8-K, 10-K, 10-Q feed since 1994

    SEC EDGAR filings (8-K, 10-K, 10-Q, S-1, 13D, 13G, Form 4 and every other form) for any company or the whole market, live or back to 1994, from the SEC's own feeds; 8-K item codes and earnings releases (Item 2.02); links to each filing, not its text.

    $0.001 per filing · Apify Store

  • Congress Stock Trades API: US House STOCK Act since 2014

    Congress stock trades: stock, option, fund and bond trades that US House members disclose in STOCK Act Periodic Transaction Reports, from the Clerk of the House's official filings since January 2014 (no Senate); ticker, amount range, owner, days to disclose.

    $0.0018 per trade · Apify Store

Company data

  • EU Annual Reports API (ESEF): XBRL financial statements

    Normalized key financials (revenue, EBIT, EBITDA, net income, EPS, assets, equity, cash flows, margins, growth) of European listed companies from official ESEF annual reports via filings.xbrl.org; 28 countries; every value linked to its XBRL fact.

    $0.01 per run plus $0.05 per annual report; raw XBRL fact rows, if switched on, $0.0005 each · Apify Store

  • Company Financials API: UK, Denmark & Norway accounts + LEI

    Financials (revenue, profit, equity, assets, cash, employees) of private and public companies from official accounts: UK Companies House, Denmark Erhvervsstyrelsen, Norway Brønnøysund; adds the LEI from GLEIF and a source per value.

    $0.04 per company-year · Apify Store

Government & procurement

  • EU Public Tenders & Contract Awards API (TED procurement)

    EU public procurement notices from TED (Tenders Electronic Daily), the EU's official tenders journal: open calls with deadlines, contract awards with winners and values, prior information; EU, EEA and more, since 2016; filter by keyword, country, CPV code, notice type, date, EUR value.

    $0.002 per notice · Apify Store

  • Pentagon / DoD Contract Awards API: winners, amounts, tickers

    US Department of Defense contract awards of $7.5M or more the same evening they are announced, plus USAspending.gov history since FY2008 (any federal agency); winner, amount, service, NAICS/PSC, matched to a US stock ticker with SEC shares outstanding.

    $0.005 per award row · Apify Store

Data tools

  • Deduplicate & Merge Apify Datasets: remove duplicate items

    Deduplicate and merge one or more Apify datasets (or a pasted JSON array): removes duplicate items by any fields (dot paths allowed) or by the whole item, keeping the first or last occurrence; streams millions of items with constant memory.

    $0.00015 per unique item written (duplicates are free) · Apify Store

  • Google Sheets Export: send any Apify dataset to a spreadsheet

    Export any Apify dataset to Google Sheets: replace a tab or append rows, with nested fields as columns and your header kept; writes with your own Google service account key and retries Google rate limits.

    $0.00015 per row exported · Apify Store

How to call

  • Apify API (synchronous): POST https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/bosco_labs~<actor>/run-sync-get-dataset-items with header Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN> and the Actor input as the JSON body; the response is the dataset items.
  • Apify hosted MCP server: add https://mcp.apify.com?tools=bosco_labs/<actor> to your MCP client (Claude, Cursor, VS Code, ...). It loads the Actor as a tool plus get-actor-run and get-dataset-items, and signs you in with OAuth or Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>. On the default server (https://mcp.apify.com) agents can also find the Actors with search-actors and run them with call-actor.
  • Pricing: pay per event, billed to your own Apify account: you pay for rows delivered at the prices listed per Actor, and runs stop at your max charge limit.
  • Bosco Labs MCP server: bosco-labs-data-mcp on PyPI (https://pypi.org/project/bosco-labs-data-mcp/) and the official MCP Registry (io.github.PB7-debug/bosco-labs-data): one MCP server for all data Actors. Run it with uvx bosco-labs-data-mcp and set APIFY_TOKEN; source in https://github.com/PB7-debug/bosco-labs-data/tree/main/mcp.
