Weather Forecast API: hourly + daily by city or lat/lon

Coming soon to the Store

Weather forecasts for place names or coordinates, up to 10 days ahead: hourly temperature, precipitation, wind speed, direction and gusts, cloud cover, humidity and pressure, plus a daily summary in local time. The data come from MET Norway, the Norwegian national weather service (the forecasts behind yr.no), which covers the whole world. Built for AI agents planning travel, logistics, events, farming or energy work, and for people who need a clean forecast table.

Input fields

FieldTypeMeaning
locationsarray (required)Places to forecast, one per line: a town or city name, optionally with a country or region after a comma ("Amsterdam", "Paris, FR", "Portland, Oregon"), or coordinates as "lat,lon" in decimal degrees, latitude first ("52.3676,4.9041"; rounded to 4 decimals). Place names come from a built-in list of towns of 15,000+ people and capitals; for anything else (a farm, a venue, a wind turbine) give coordinates. Up to 50; repeats are forecast once. One output row per location. Required.
hoursintegerHow far ahead to forecast, counted from the current hour, e.g. 48 for today and tomorrow. Max 240 (10 days; MET Norway's forecast ends after about 9.5). Steps are hourly for the first ~2.5 days, then every 6 hours. Default (or empty) = 48.
timezonestringIANA timezone for output times and daily summaries, e.g. "Europe/Amsterdam", "America/New_York", "UTC". Empty (default) = the place's own timezone for place names, UTC for coordinates.
summarybooleantrue (default) = a daily summary for every day in hours plus only the next 12 hourly steps (about 7 KB per location). false = the daily summary plus every forecast step in hours (about 18 KB per location for 48 hours, 33 KB for 240). The price is the same.

Sources, licence and limits

Where does the data come from?

  • Forecast: MET Norway's Locationforecast 2.0 (api.met.no). Outside the Nordic region it is based on the ECMWF global model. In the Nordic region MET uses its own high-resolution model, post-processed with local observations.
  • Place names: GeoNames.
  • Sources per row: source_url is the exact MET request; place_source_url is the GeoNames record.

How fresh is it? MET Norway updates Nordic forecasts every hour and the rest of the world every 6 hours. Their terms forbid asking for the same point again within 30 minutes (Nordic) or 3 hours (elsewhere), and before the response's Expires time. So this Actor keeps each forecast in a key-value store named weather-forecast-cache in your Apify account, and serves it from there until it may be refreshed. fetched_at shows when it was fetched.

What is not included?

  • Past weather or observations; only current forecasts.
  • Weather warnings and alerts.
  • Air quality, marine and aviation (above-ground) forecasts.
  • Wind at hub height. wind_speed is at 10 m above ground; for turbines, scale it up yourself.
  • Gusts and precipitation probability outside the Nordic region.
  • Forecasts beyond about 9.5 days. MET's forecast ends there, even with hours: 240.

Why is the daily precipitation off by a little at midnight? After the first ~2.5 days, steps are 6 hours long. A 6-hour period counts toward the local day it starts in, so in timezones that are not a multiple of 6 hours from UTC some rain can land on the neighbouring day.

My town isn't found. The built-in list holds places with 15,000+ people and capitals. Give the coordinates instead ("lat,lon"). Unknown places are free.

Limits?

  • 50 locations per run.
  • MET Norway is a free public service without an SLA. If it does not answer, the row is marked source_error and not charged. If a recent cached forecast exists (under 12 hours old), it is served with a warning instead.
