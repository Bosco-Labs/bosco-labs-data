Sources, licence and limits

Which countries? All 28 countries in the filings.xbrl.org index, listed above. Germany is not in the index (0 filings). Most testing was done on NL, BE and FR reports; reports from countries with their own IFRS taxonomy variant (e.g. Ukraine) may have fewer standard fields filled. metrics_found tells you how complete a row is.

How fresh? Reports appear a few days to a few weeks after publication, once filings.xbrl.org has processed them.

Why are some fields empty? Companies tag their statements differently. Some use their own (extension) concepts instead of the standard IFRS ones; those stay null rather than being guessed. For example, LVMH tags no standard operating profit, so its operating margin and EBITDA are empty too.

Number of employees? ESEF reports almost never tag the headcount as a number (it sits in text notes), so it isn't included. employee_benefits_expense (total staff costs) is.

Are the numbers in thousands? No. All values are in full currency units (XBRL canonical values), per-share figures are per share, and ratios are fractions (0.25 = 25%).

Legal basis? These are public regulatory disclosures, and filings.xbrl.org currently places no restrictions on use. Raw fact rows can contain names from notes such as directors' remuneration; if you process those, you are responsible for your own legal basis under GDPR.
