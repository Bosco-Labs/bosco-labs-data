Company Check API: identity, financials & SEC filings in 1 call

On Apify Store

One call answers "who is this company and what are its numbers?": legal name, LEI, registration number, status, legal form, registered address and (France, Finland, Estonia) industry and registration date, the key figures of its last fiscal years (revenue, operating profit, net income, equity, assets, cash, employees), the US listing and latest SEC filings, for UK companies the current directors and owners (persons with significant control), for Polish companies the board, proxies, shareholders and bankruptcy/restructuring entries from the National Court Register (KRS), and for US banks the FDIC record. Every value links to the official record it came from. Built for AI agents doing research, KYB or due diligence, and for people who want a quick, sourced company profile.

Input fields

querystring (required)The company to check: a name (e.g. "Novo Nordisk") or an identifier: LEI (20 characters, e.g. "724500Y6DUVHQD6OXN27"), UK company number ("00445790", "SC123456"), Danish CVR ("24256790"), Norwegian org number ("923609016"), French SIREN (9 digits, "552032534") or SIRET (14 digits, "55203253400703"), Finnish Business ID with its dash ("0112038-9"), Estonian registry code (8 digits, "12417834"), US ticker in capitals or with $ ("AAPL", "$brk.b"; foreign companies listed in the US resolve to their home country), SEC CIK ("320193") or FDIC certificate of a US bank ("FDIC:628"). includearrayWhich sections to fill: any of "identity", "financials", "us" (SEC filings of US issuers), "people" (UK officers and owners from Companies House; Polish board, proxies and shareholders from KRS), and the due-diligence sections "charges" (UK registered mortgages and secured debt), "appointments" (each current UK officer's other company appointments; also fills "people") and "insolvency" (UK: insolvency notices in The Gazette; Poland: bankruptcy, restructuring and liquidation entries from KRS), e.g. ["identity", "charges", "insolvency"]. Default (or empty) = identity, financials, us and people, plus insolvency for Polish companies (free from the KRS extract); the UK due-diligence sections only when listed. Why is financials empty? See not_found: the company may file no machine-readable accounts (paper/PDF filings, very small companies, countries without an open accounts register), or it is a US company that files no 10-K (US banks: see bank.financials). Unlisted French companies are not covered: INPI's annual-accounts API needs an account, and the open INPI-derived "Ratios financiers" dataset files figures under the wrong fiscal year (checked against Danone's ESEF reports), so it is not used. Most Finnish companies file paper or PDF accounts, which are not open data.

Why is revenue null? The company didn't tag it: small UK and Danish companies often publish no profit and loss account or only gross profit. employees is usually null for ESEF reports, which rarely tag it.

A name gave the wrong company. Names are matched against GLEIF's register of legal entities (companies with an LEI; with country NO, FR or FI also the national register). When several entities share the name, a listed company (one filing ESEF reports) goes first, and candidates and a warning say there was a choice; without country that happens for any name shared by several entities. Check candidates, add country, or pass the LEI or registration number, which always resolve exactly. Write a US ticker in capitals (COKE) or with $; Coke is searched as a name.

Are figures consolidated? ESEF reports (listed companies) are consolidated group figures. National register accounts are the company's own statutory accounts; for Norway they are explicitly the company's own accounts, not the group's. financials.basis says which.

Insider buys and sells? This version counts Form 4 filings in the last 90 days (buys and sells together). For each transaction, use SEC Insider Trades.

Need more? Full statements with 16-25 metrics per year: Company Financials API (UK, DK, NO) and EU Annual Reports API (ESEF). Every SEC filing: SEC Filings Feed.

Personal data? Company-level data, plus one exception for KYB: for UK companies the people section lists the current officers and persons with significant control as Companies House publishes them (name, role, appointment or notification date, nationality, country of residence, month and year of birth, nature of control). Their addresses and occupations are never output, and resigned officers and ceased PSCs are left out; leave people out of include to skip it. The opt-in appointments section adds each current officer's other company appointments (company, role, dates), read live and never stored; charges lists the lenders named in each charge; insolvency outputs notice type, date, company name and link only, never the people named in a notice. For Polish companies people lists the board, proxies and shareholders exactly as the Ministry of Justice's open KRS API serves them: people's names already masked to a first letter and asterisks, never a PESEL, no marital fields, no free-text proxy scopes; trustees and liquidators are not listed. Polish data is read live per call and not stored. Outside the UK and Poland, names of directors, officers, board members, auditors or shareholders are never requested or output (the French register's dirigeants are never requested; Finland's open data has no persons, and the address's c/o line is dropped; the Estonian persons, shareholder and beneficial-owner files are never downloaded). Estonian sole traders (FIE) are left out of the index and name search, and a lookup by their code fails without a charge. Sole traders are natural persons: Norwegian ones are excluded from name search, French ones (entrepreneurs individuels) from name search and from lookups by SIREN, which fail without a charge; Finland's open data holds none. Sole proprietors with an LEI (GLEIF category SOLE_PROPRIETOR, any country) are never candidates and are refused without a charge.

How fresh is it? Live: every call reads the sources directly (GLEIF is updated several times a day, registers when companies file). Exception: Estonia's legal form, VAT number, activity and figures come from a weekly index of the register's daily open-data files (the date is in sources[].name); name, status and address are read live. FDIC figures are quarterly (Call Reports).
