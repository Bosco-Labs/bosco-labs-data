Input fields

FieldTypeMeaning
periodstring (required)Calendar period in SEC frames format: "CY2025" = annual figures, "CY2025Q3" = 3-month quarter figures. Required, 2009 to the current year. Calendar-aligned: each company comes with its own fiscal period that best fits it (e.g. CY2025 holds Apple's fiscal year to Sep 2025, Microsoft's to Jun 2025, Walmart's to Jan 2026), shown in period_start/period_end. Balance-sheet metrics are taken at each company's period end.
filtersarrayConditions every company must meet, as a list of {metric, min, max} (either bound optional, both inclusive), e.g. [{"metric": "revenue", "min": 10000000000}, {"metric": "net_margin", "min": 0.2}] = revenue of at least $10B and net margin of at least 20%. Money in full USD (10000000000 = $10B), ratios as fractions (0.2 = 20%), EPS in USD per share. A company without a value for a filter metric is left out. Metrics: see sortBy. Empty = no filter (all companies with a sortBy value).
sortBystringMetric to rank by, e.g. "operating_cash_flow". Same names as the SEC Company Financials Actor; the last seven (free_cash_flow to equity_ratio) are derived from two reported metrics. Companies without a value for it are left out. Empty = the first filter's metric, else revenue.
sortOrderstring"desc" = largest first (top N), "asc" = smallest first (e.g. the lowest net margin). Default "desc".
maxResultsintegerMost companies to return (and charge for), e.g. 20 for a top 20. The status message says how many matched in total. Default 50, max 1000.
metricsarrayMore fields to show per company besides the sortBy and filter metrics, e.g. ["operating_cash_flow", "total_assets"]. Same names as sortBy. Each adds a few SEC requests. Empty = only the sortBy and filter metrics (plus the inputs of derived ones).
tickersOnlybooleantrue = skip companies without a ticker in the SEC's company_tickers.json (delisted companies, private issuers of public debt, subsidiaries). Default false. E.g. true.
includeSourcesbooleantrue = each row has sources: per value the XBRL tag, the SEC accession number and the filing URL (or the formula for derived values). false = values only, about 3x smaller rows. Default true.

Sources, licence and limits

Which companies? Every SEC filer whose XBRL facts are in the frames: about 6,000 companies with annual revenue or net income for 2025, including some without a ticker (delisted companies, private issuers of public debt); tickersOnly drops those. Values are in USD only: companies reporting in another currency (many 20-F filers, e.g. in EUR or DKK) are not in USD frames, and the SEC publishes no frames for IFRS tags, so most IFRS filers are missing. Use the SEC Company Financials Actor for them.

How fresh? The SEC updates frames shortly after filings. A calendar year fills up as annual reports come in, up to about three months after each company's year end (and later for fiscal years ending early the next year), so screens of the latest year miss late filers.

Why is a company missing? It didn't tag the metric with one of the standard candidate tags, reported it for a period that doesn't fit the calendar period, has no value for a filter or the sort metric, or reports in another currency. Banks have no gross profit, operating profit or current assets; most companies report quarterly cash flow only year to date, so quarterly (CY2025Q3) cash-flow fields are usually null except for a company's first fiscal quarter. Calendar-year companies report no separate fourth quarter (the 10-K has only the full year), so a Q4 period (CY2025Q4) holds mostly companies with other fiscal years: use CY2025 instead.

Fiscal year or calendar year? Calendar-aligned, see above: each company appears with the fiscal period that best fits the calendar period. For exact fiscal years and quarters of a given company, use the SEC Company Financials Actor.

What is not included? Market data (prices, market cap, P/E), revenue growth, segment figures, non-GAAP figures, forecasts, and anything before 2009.

Legal basis? SEC filings are US government public records. See LEGAL.md.
