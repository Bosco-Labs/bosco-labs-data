Sanctions List Screening API: OFAC, UK, EU, UN & US lists

On Apify Store

Answers "is this company, vessel or person sanctioned?" for one or many names, against the official OFAC SDN and non-SDN lists, the US Consolidated Screening List (Commerce Entity List, Denied Persons, State lists), the UK Sanctions List, the EU financial sanctions list and the UN Security Council list. Sources, licence and limits

Is this legal advice or a compliance decision? No. It is a screening aid. Fuzzy name matching produces false positives (a namesake) and can miss a party listed under a spelling or script it doesn't recognise. Review every match against the official entry (source_url) before acting, and use your own judgement or counsel for decisions. Only the official publications are authoritative (for the EU, the Official Journal).

The CSL's Treasury sub-lists are skipped because the same entries come from OFAC directly.

What is not covered? National lists of other countries, PEPs (politically exposed persons), adverse media, ownership (the OFAC 50% rule: companies owned by listed parties are often not listed themselves) and EU sectoral or vessel annexes outside the financial sanctions list are not covered.

Does spelling matter? Less than you would think: word order and legal forms are ignored, accents are folded, a typo or transliteration difference ("Sovkomflot", "Gasprom") still scores above 85, and a name written as two words finds the one-word listing ("Gazprom Bank" finds GAZPROMBANK, "Hua Wei" finds HUAWEI). Common words are a different matter: "National Oil Company" or "John Smith" match every listing that contains those words, by design, so compare countries, identifiers and dates before acting on a contains match.

How fresh is it? Each run uses copies of the lists fetched at most 1 hour earlier; older copies are downloaded again. On the API a copy up to 12 hours old answers the call. When a download fails, a copy up to 2 days old is used and the warning says so; an older copy is not used, that list counts as not checked and the name is free. lists_checked shows each list's own publish date. Agencies publish on their own schedule (OFAC and the CSL often daily, the UK and EU when designations change).

Why is listing_date null for OFAC? OFAC's standard XML doesn't include designation dates (only its 110 MB "enhanced" file does). Check the OFAC entry page in source_url.

Personal data? The lists name individuals. This Actor serves only per-name screening results (at most 5 listings per name, no bulk export) for sanctions compliance, and keeps only the fields needed to confirm or rule out a match. See LEGAL.md.
