Sources, licence and limits

One row per award or per transaction? Per award. An award (contract, grant, loan) gets many actions over its life: the base award and its modifications. A row shows the award's largest action in your window (action_amount_usd, action_date, modification_number) next to its lifetime totals. If award_date_signed is before your window, the row is a modification of an older award, not a new win.

How fresh is the data? Civilian agencies report within days. Department of Defense contract actions are published up to 90 days after signing (a DoD rule), so recent DoD windows are nearly empty; for same-evening DoD awards use our Pentagon / DoD Contract Awards Actor.

What does minAmount filter on? The award's total obligated amount, not the single action.

Why is an amount 0 or negative? Administrative modifications obligate $0, de-obligations are negative, and loan actions obligate $0 (the face value is in total_value_usd).

How does ticker matching work? The recipient name and its parent company (from the award record) are cleaned (lower case, no Inc/Corp/LLC/Holdings) and must exactly equal the cleaned name of a NYSE, Nasdaq or CBOE company in the SEC's ticker list. We would rather miss a match than give a wrong ticker: joint ventures, foreign parents, renamed subsidiaries and private companies stay null. It is not investment advice.

Are individuals included? No. USAspending redacts financial assistance to individuals ("REDACTED DUE TO PII") and groups small payments into county-level "MULTIPLE RECIPIENTS" records; we leave both out. Some contracts go to sole proprietors whose business name is a person's name; those are shown exactly as USAspending publishes them, with nothing added.

Why does a keyword match an award whose description lacks it? USAspending's keyword search covers more text fields than the description shown.

What is not included? Sub-awards, IDV vehicles themselves (their task orders are included), DUNS numbers, recipient street addresses and executive pay. Search history starts at 2007-10-01. A run scans at most 2,000 actions; narrow the filters for rare matches.
