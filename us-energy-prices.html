Input fields

FieldTypeMeaning
seriesarrayWhich series to fetch, by plain name (case-insensitive), up to 20 per run, e.g. ["gasoline_retail:CA", "crude_stocks", "electricity_price:TX:RES"]. Names: gasoline_retail[:<area>] and diesel_retail[:<area>] (USD/gallon, weekly; area US (default), PADD1-PADD5, or CA, CO, FL, MA, MN, NY, OH, TX, WA; diesel US, PADD1-5, CA), electricity_price[:<state>[:<sector>]] (cents/kWh, monthly; state US (default) or a 2-letter code; sector ALL (default), RES, COM, IND, TRA), crude_stocks, spr_stocks, gasoline_stocks, distillate_stocks (thousand barrels, weekly), crude_production (thousand barrels/day, weekly), gas_storage (Bcf, weekly), refinery_utilization (%, weekly). Sources, licence and limits

How fresh is it? Live: every call reads the EIA API. Retail gasoline and diesel prices are surveyed on Mondays and published Monday evening or Tuesday. Petroleum stocks, production and refinery use come from the Weekly Petroleum Status Report (Wednesdays), gas storage from the Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report (Thursdays). Monthly electricity prices lag about two months. Federal holidays shift the schedule.

Why no WTI or Brent price? See "Not included" above: those are licensed vendor prices.

Why no diesel price for Texas? EIA's weekly diesel survey covers the US, the five PADD regions and California only; other areas come back as a free error row.

Do I need an EIA API key? No. We call EIA with our own key; it never appears in the output or logs.

Can I republish the values? EIA data is US government work in the public domain. Name EIA as the source (each row has an attribution), don't present changed values as EIA's, and don't imply EIA endorses you. Our change field is marked as computed by Bosco Labs.

Personal data? None. National, regional and state aggregates only.
