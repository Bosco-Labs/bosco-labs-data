What are the top holdings of the ARK Innovation ETF (ARKK)?

The largest positions of the ARK Innovation ETF from its newest Form N-PORT filing at the SEC, with shares, value, portfolio share and change in shares.

Answered bySEC 13F Holdings API: fund & ETF holdings, 13D/13G, Form 144
Price$0.0018 per row, plus $0.01 per "who holds this stock" search (13F or N-PORT data set)
StatusOn Apify Store

ARK Innovation ETF reports its holdings to the SEC on Form N-PORT; this input returns its largest positions from the newest filing, with shares, value_usd, portfolio_pct, CUSIP, ISIN and ticker, and the change in shares against the previous report. Each row links the N-PORT filing on EDGAR.

Tool input

{
  "mode": "holdingsNport",
  "fund": "ARKK",
  "maxResults": 25
}

Call the Apify API

Runs the Actor and returns the rows in one request, billed to your Apify account ($0.0018 per row, plus $0.01 per "who holds this stock" search (13F or N-PORT data set)).

curl https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/bosco_labs~sec-ownership/run-sync-get-dataset-items \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"mode": "holdingsNport", "fund": "ARKK", "maxResults": 25}'
