  "query": "stikstof",

  ],

Runs the Actor and returns the rows in one request, billed to your Apify account ($0.005 per decision returned).

  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"query": "stikstof", "courts": ["RVS"], "dateFrom": "2025-01-01", "maxResults": 20}'
