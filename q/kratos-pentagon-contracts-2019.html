Which Kratos contracts did the Pentagon award in 2019, and how large were they?

Answered byPentagon / DoD Contract Awards API: winners, amounts, tickers
Price$0.005 per award row
StatusComing soon to the Store

tickers matches award recipients to the listed company (here Kratos Defense, KTOS) and its subsidiaries. For 2019 the rows come from USAspending.gov history, filtered to the Department of Defense and awards of at least $7.5 million (the defaults of agencies and minAmountUsd). Each row has award_id, action_date, amount_usd, awarding_sub_agency (Army, Navy, Air Force ...), naics_code, description and the matched ticker.

Tool input

{
  "tickers": [
    "KTOS"
  ],
  "dateFrom": "2019-01-01",
  "dateTo": "2019-12-31"
}
