What does Article 50 of the AI Act require today, and has it been amended? Quote the text and link the source.

The current text of Article 50 of the AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) from EUR-Lex, with any amending acts and the in-force status of the regulation.

Answered byEU Law Lookup API (EUR-Lex): article text, in force, amendments
Price$0.005 per act or article returned
StatusOn Apify Store

The Actor resolves "AI Act" to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on EUR-Lex and returns the article from the latest consolidated version: article_title, article_text, and article_amended_by if the article was changed. The act-level fields say whether it is in_force, its date_entry_into_force and the CELEX and ELI identifiers, with eur_lex_url and source_url. Consolidated texts are a documentation tool; only the Official Journal is authentic.

Tool input

{
  "query": "AI Act",
  "article": "50"
}

Add the Apify MCP server with this tool to your MCP client (Claude, Cursor, VS Code, ...); it signs you in with OAuth or Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>.

Call the Apify API

Runs the Actor and returns the rows in one request, billed to your Apify account ($0.005 per act or article returned).

  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"query": "AI Act", "article": "50"}'
