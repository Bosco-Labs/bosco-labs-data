When to pick something else

  • You need option grants, exercises or other derivative transactions: our insider tool reads only the non-derivative table of Form 4.
  • You want a flat monthly price instead of paying per row: check each tool's Store page for its current pricing model.
  • You need Senate trades: we do not cover the Senate (our congressional tool covers the House only).
