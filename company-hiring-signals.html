Sources, licence and limits

Which companies are covered? Companies whose public job board runs on Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby: many tech and venture-backed companies (Stripe, Airbnb, Figma, Spotify, Palantir, Notion, Ramp, ...). Companies on Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable, iCIMS, SuccessFactors or their own careers site are not covered and come back as found: false at no charge.

A name gave no board or the wrong one. Board names don't always match the company name. Open the company's careers page, follow a job link, and pass that URL (e.g. https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/airbnb) or greenhouse:airbnb.

Why is by_country short, or remote null? Greenhouse has no country or workplace field: remote is true only when the location text says "remote", and countries are read from the location text ("US-Remote", "Dublin, Ireland"; a bare "Seattle" has none). Lever gives ISO country codes (GB), Ashby country names (United Kingdom).

What do departments look like? Each company names its own. Some use broad names ("Engineering"), some internal codes (Stripe: "Tech" with teams like "8217 Risk Engineering"). For Greenhouse, department is the top-level department and team the one the job sits in. Filtering on title keywords is often more reliable across companies.

What does posted_at mean? Greenhouse: first published; Lever: when the posting was created; Ashby: when it was last published. Postings are as the company publishes them; the Actor doesn't check whether a role is really still open.

Are job descriptions or salaries included? No. Rows hold titles, departments, locations, dates and links; descriptions stay on the linked posting page.

Personal data? Company-level data only. No recruiter or hiring-manager names or emails are collected or output.

How fresh is it? Live: every run reads the job boards directly.
