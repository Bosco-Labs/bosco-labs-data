Counterparty Check API: KYB checklist from official registers

Can I do business with this company? One call checks a supplier, customer or partner against official sources and returns a checklist: pass, fail, review or unverified per check, each with its source link and time. Built for AI agents doing KYB, vendor onboarding or "may I pay this party?" checks, and for people who want a fast, sourced first screen. To screen a vendor list, pass up to 10 companies in companies: one checklist per company, same price each.

On the HTTP API, MCP and x402 an API key gets up to 20 such free partial answers per UTC day (x402 payers share one such allowance); after that such a company is not checked and not charged (status: "failed", no sanctions screen result: sanctions_hits: null; the message says when the count resets at 00:00 UTC). The free sanctions screen of an ambiguous name or of a company no source knows counts as one of these answers too; past the limit that name is not screened (sanctions_hits: null). Charged answers are never limited. A check that is unverified because the register does not hold that data for the country, or because you gave no VAT number, is a complete answer and is charged.

Input fields

FieldTypeMeaning
querystringThe counterparty to check: a name (e.g. "Novo Nordisk") or an identifier: LEI (20 characters, e.g. "724500Y6DUVHQD6OXN27"), UK company number ("00445790", "SC123456"), Danish CVR ("24256790"), Norwegian org number ("923609016"), French SIREN (9 digits, "552032534") or SIRET (14 digits, "55203253400703"), Finnish Business ID with its dash ("0112038-9"), Estonian registry code (8 digits, "12417834"), Irish CRO company number ("CRO 104547", or the digits with country IE), Czech IČO ("IČO 27074358", the company's VAT number "CZ27074358", or the digits with country CZ), Latvian registration number (11 digits, "40003032949"), US ticker in capitals or with $ ("AAPL", "$brk.b"; foreign companies listed in the US resolve to their home country), SEC CIK ("320193"). The type is detected from format and check digits; an 8-digit number valid in several registers (UK, Danish CVR, Estonian) is settled by asking them, and country decides when given. Required, unless you give a companies list instead.
countrystringISO 3166 alpha-2 country code to disambiguate, e.g. "DK", "GB", "NO", "FR", "FI", "EE", "IE", "CZ", "LV", "NL", "US". With a name it limits the search to that country and, for NO, FR, FI, EE, IE, CZ and LV, also searches the national register (most companies there have no LEI); with a number it says which register the number belongs to (e.g. "GB" for a UK number without a leading zero, "EE" for an Estonian registry code, "FI" for a Business ID typed without its dash, "IE" for an Irish CRO number, "CZ" for a Czech IČO). Empty = detect from the query.
vatNumberstringThe VAT number the counterparty gave you, with its country prefix, e.g. "DK24256790". Checked live in VIES (valid or not, registered name) and matched against the register's name and country. Empty = the VAT number the register lists, if any (Estonia, Poland, Czech Republic); otherwise the VAT check is unverified.
ibanstringThe bank account the counterparty wants to be paid on, e.g. "DK5000400440116243". Checked offline: length, layout and ISO 13616 check digits, bank code, and whether its country matches the company's. No bank is contacted, so it does not prove who holds the account.
walletAddressesarrayWallet addresses the counterparty wants to be paid on, e.g. ["0x7FF9cFad3877F21d41Da833E2F775dB0569eE3D9"]. Each is matched exactly against the addresses OFAC lists. At most 10.
companiesarrayCheck a list of companies in one run instead of query: up to 10 companies, each a name or number ("Tesco PLC") or an object with the fields above, e.g. ["Tesco PLC", {"query": "Novo Nordisk A/S", "country": "DK", "vatNumber": "DK24256790"}, {"query": "00445790", "country": "GB"}]. One row per company, priced like a single check ($0.05 per company checked); an ambiguous name, or a company no source knows or that does not answer in time, is a row that costs nothing. Sources, licence and limits

Is this a compliance decision? No. It is a screening aid built from official sources. Fuzzy name matching gives false positives (namesakes) and can miss a name written another way. Review every fail and review against the linked source, and use your own judgement or counsel for decisions.

Why is a check unverified? The source does not cover that country (see the coverage table), the source did not answer in time, or the input to check is missing (no VAT number). The detail says which. When a source did not answer, the company is not charged.

Does the IBAN check prove who holds the account? No. It checks that the number is well formed and in the expected country; no bank is contacted.

How fresh is it? Registers, GLEIF, VIES and SEC EDGAR are read live. Sanctions lists: this Actor uses a copy at most 90 minutes old, the API one at most 12 hours old; each list's own date is in the detail. When a list cannot be refreshed, a copy up to 2 days old is used with a warning; an older one is not used, and the company is then not charged. UK insolvency notices come from a nightly copy of The Gazette; when that copy cannot be read, a narrower live search stands in, the insolvency check is unverified and the company is not charged.

Personal data? Officer and owner names come from the public registers and are screened only against sanctions lists, for this check. Nothing about the people is stored. See LEGAL.md.
