What you get

schema_versionstringOutput schema version.
querystringThe VAT number or IBAN as given.
check_typestringvat or iban.
numberstring | nullNormalised number: upper case without spaces, e.g. IE6388047V or NL91ABNA0417164300 (Greek VAT numbers with EL).
country_codestring | nullCountry of the number: VAT prefix (EL = Greece, XI = Northern Ireland) or IBAN country (ISO 3166 alpha-2); null if unreadable.
statusstringpass = valid; fail = invalid (malformed, or VIES says not valid); unverified = VIES or the member state gave no answer (never means invalid; check again later).

registered_namestring | nullName registered with the VAT number, as VIES returns it; null when the member state does not share it (e.g. DE) or the number is not valid.
registered_addressstring | nullRegistered address as VIES returns it (lines joined with commas); null when not shared.

Input fields

FieldTypeMeaning
vatNumbersarrayEU VAT numbers to check live against VIES, each with its country prefix, e.g. ["IE6388047V", "NL004495445B01"]. Spaces, dots and dashes are ignored; GR is read as EL (Greece), XI is Northern Ireland. 0-10 numbers, checked one at a time (about 1-3 s each). Empty = no VAT check.
ibansarrayIBANs to validate (check digits, length and layout per country, bank code), in electronic or print format, e.g. ["NL91ABNA0417164300", "DE89 3704 0044 0532 0130 00"]. 0-50 IBANs; no bank or third-party service is called. Empty = no IBAN check.
expectedNamestringThe company name you expect behind the VAT numbers, e.g. "Google Ireland Ltd". Compared with the name VIES returns after removing case, accents, punctuation and legal forms (Ltd = Limited, B.V., GmbH ...): name_match true or false; null when the member state shares no name (e.g. Germany). Empty = no name check.
expectedCountrystringISO 3166 alpha-2 code of the country you expect, e.g. "NL". Compared with each IBAN's and VAT number's country (country_match; GB IBANs also match JE, GG and IM, FR IBANs the French overseas territories). Empty = no country check.
requesterVatNumberstringYour own EU VAT number, e.g. "NL123456789B01". VIES then returns a consultation number (consultation_number) that proves to a tax authority that you checked a number at a given time. Empty = no consultation number.

Sources, licence and limits

Where does the VAT answer come from? VIES, the European Commission's VAT Information Exchange System, which asks the tax administration of the member state in real time. The Commission accepts no responsibility for the data, which comes from national databases. A VIES answer is not legal or tax advice and does not by itself give a right to a VAT exemption.

What does "unverified" mean? VIES or that member state's database did not answer (maintenance, outage or a request limit). It is not "invalid" and costs nothing; check again later. The Actor never retries more than once and never sends numbers in parallel.

Why is a real VAT number "fail"? Some member states only list numbers registered for intra-EU trade in VIES. "Not valid" means not valid in VIES; the company can ask its tax administration to update VIES.

Does a valid IBAN mean the account exists? No. It means the number is well-formed for its country and the check digits are right, which catches typos and invented numbers. Whether the account is open and whose it is only the bank knows. bank_code is the bank identifier at the position the SWIFT IBAN Registry gives for that country.

Do you store the numbers or answers? No. VIES answers are returned to you only, not cached or reused, and numbers are not logged.

Which countries? VAT: the 27 EU member states and Northern Ireland (XI); not the UK (GB), Norway or Switzerland, which VIES does not cover. IBAN: all 89 countries in the SWIFT IBAN Registry.

Limits: 10 VAT numbers and 50 IBANs per run; about 1-3 seconds per VAT number.
